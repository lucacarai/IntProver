Proposition letters
p, q, p1, q12

Negation goes first: neg p or neg neg p. Use neg (p and q) to negate a group.

Repeated and or or is fine. Use parentheses when mixing them or repeating imp or iff.

iff binds least tightly: p imp q iff r means (p imp q) iff r.
